About

RetinaCare AI helps screen retinal fundus photographs for diabetic retinopathy (DR). It supports, and never replaces, a clinician's judgement. Every result needs specialist review.

How an image is processed

  1. Upload
  2. Quality assessment
  3. Quality check (a poor image stops only that image)
  4. Preprocessing
  5. EfficientNet-B0
  6. 5-class DR classification
  7. Confidence
  8. Safety status
  9. Result
  10. Specialist review

Model Information

Architecture
EfficientNet-B0
Classes
0 No DR, 1 Mild, 2 Moderate, 3 Severe, 4 Proliferative
